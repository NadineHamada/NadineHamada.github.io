how much you can extract/learn from something?

 what kind of physical system is actually carrying/encoding the information: classical bits, quantum states (qubits), entangled quantum states, etc.
" physical substrates" / "different media"= Quantum channel, classical channel, entanglement-assisted channel 

The unifying principle: 
information extractable from any process can never exceed the **entropy reduction** that process is physically capable of producing — classical or quantum, lossy or lossless, entanglement-assisted or not. Each named theorem is just that one idea, re-derived for a different physical substrate.

# Medium 
midim can blund a lot of meaning in machine learning 

1 — Physical substrate:  
What physical carrier? 
Answer : classical bit(no coherence) vs. qubit(with coherence).

Coherence:
"quantum information" is a proxy word for the physical resource: "Coherence".
Quantum information basically means coherence. 
A quantum state ∣ψ⟩=α∣0⟩+β∣1⟩ isn't just "some probability of being 0, some probability of being 1" — it has a definite relative phase between 
α and β. That relative phase is coherence.
When people say "preserve the quantum state",what they're really asking for is: keep the phase relationships between the amplitudes intact.
Lose the phase, and what's left is just a classical probability distribution wearing quantum notation — the "quantumness" is gone.

2- ... :
What kind of information is being sent?
Answer: Classical information (a message drawn from some alphabet) vs. quantum information (an actual quantum state you're trying to preserve/transmit).+ coherence mention 

Explanation:
a qubit(with Coherence)(quantum physical substrate) can carry either classical messages (Holevo's setting) 
                                                                or   quantum states (needs the qubit's **coherence** to actually be preserved end-to-end).
classical bit(no coherence) (classical physical substrate) can only ever carry classical information — there's no coherence to lose.


3- Protocol Level Assumption layered on top:... : 

answer: None, pre-shared entanglement between sender and receiver
        or
        classical/quantum feedback from receiver to sender(a less commonly named variant)

This changes the achievable rate without changing either the Physical substrate(axe1:What physical carrier? ) or ... (axe2:what's being sent?)


4 — Fidelity/error criterion: 
How strict does the reconstruction have to be? 

answer: 
vanishing error only in the asymptotic limit of many uses (Shannon's and Holevo's standard setting), 
bounded distortion allowed even asymptotically (rate-distortion),
exact zero-error with no asymptotic slack at all (Shannon's separate zero-error capacity theory, which turns into a graph-coloring problem via the Lovász theta function).
