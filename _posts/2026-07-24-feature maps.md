# Feature Maps 
- **Feature map** \(\phi\): the function that performs the embedding.
  - **Classical:** typically either **deterministic** or **learned**.
  - **Quantum:** implemented as a state-preparation circuit,
    \[
    x \mapsto |\phi(x)\rangle.
    \]
